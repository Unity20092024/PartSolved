# Approved photography slots

No licensed real product photography was supplied for Milestone 1. The application intentionally renders labeled temporary slots instead of generated hardware or unrelated stock photography.

Needed assets:

- Hero: residential window/door scene with a real black handle on the right; wide landscape composition with space for copy on the left.
- Window hardware: real sash lock/latch/operator installed at a window.
- Door hardware: real lever handle, close-up.
- Window parts: real roller assembly, close-up.
- Door parts: real metal hinge, close-up.
- Guide: real window measurement with tape measure.

Before adding assets, confirm rights to use them commercially and record the source, rights holder, permission/license, and attribution here. Do not scrape supplier sites. Set approved local image paths and descriptive alt text in src/config/categories.ts and src/config/photography.ts. Images are rendered with next/image and fixed aspect ratios.
