# Information still needed from Strybuc (and any future supplier)

- Authorized CSV/XLSX product export, field definitions and stable supplier record/SKU identifiers; keep those distinct from actual manufacturer names and part numbers.
- Manufacturer part numbers, variant groupings and documented alternate identifiers, including the meaning of punctuation and leading zeros. Confirm any claimed equivalence separately.
- Permitted hardware category/family mapping; accurate product descriptions, finish/color/material/handedness values, and unknown-value conventions.
- Named dimensions with units, measurement methodology/tolerances and manufacturer documentation; balance end fittings, screw spacing, backset and other family-specific specifications where available.
- Explicit evidence for compatibility/interchangeability claims, applicable model/series/revisions, verification dates, restrictions and withdrawal/correction process. A visually similar photo is not evidence of exact replacement fit.
- Authorized product photographs and technical drawings or approved media delivery method, written reuse permissions/license terms, required attribution, modification/cropping restrictions and expiry/revocation rules. No scraping is assumed. Current uploads support raster JPEG/PNG/WebP; SVG/PDF handling would require a separately reviewed safe path.
- Approved live product/purchasing URLs and allowed domains. Do not provide test checkout destinations for customer routing.
- Confirmed purchasing/affiliate arrangement, approved tracking format and attribution/disclosure requirements. No affiliate links or tags will be invented.
- Availability definitions and update cadence, discontinuation/replacement data with provenance, last-verified dates, delta/update/delete semantics, and how errors are corrected. Availability is unknown until sourced.
- File delivery cadence/access authorization and retention requirements. This foundation has manual reviewed import batches, not a presumed live supplier API or scheduled scraper.

Before public launch: owner approval for the candidate shared migration, hosted Auth/RLS/private Storage/persistence checks, reviewed actual supplier data and media permissions, and a separate Production release approval. AI family recognition and future exact catalog matching remain independent stages.
